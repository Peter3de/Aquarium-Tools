My tank:

Working volume is an estimate. It uses a flat 10% allowance for rock and sand and does not account for the water actually displaced by your rock, substrate and equipment. If you know your real system volume, enter it as total litres. Your tank settings are saved on this device. Choose the coral group above to match your tank. Ranges come from the Reef Coral Guide.

Tank parameters

ParameterYour readingIdealToleratedStatus

Ideal is where to aim. Tolerated is what the coral can survive for short periods, so a reading outside it is flagged as very low or very high. Stability matters more than hitting exact numbers: avoid swings in alkalinity of more than 1 dKH per day, and in temperature of more than 1 °C per day.
