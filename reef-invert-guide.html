No copper. Never use copper-based medications or treatments in a tank with inverts, and check the label of any fish medication first. Copper is toxic to snails, shrimp, crabs, urchins, clams and worms even at low levels, and it can linger in rock and sand after treatment. Treat sick fish in a separate hospital tank.
